Darma Valley ka Sabse Khaas Dera

Darma Ganga nadi ke kinare, Panchachuli glaciers ke seedhe saamne — Darma Heights Cottage ek aisa homestay hai jahan aap pahadi zindagi ko asli roop mein jeete hain. Hum Homestay, Zostel, Trekking, Camping sab karte hain.

Yahan na sirf ek raat rukne ki jagah milti hai — balki Darma Valley ki 12 villages ki sanskriti, Bhotiya community ka swagat, aur Himalaya ka raw, unfiltered anubhav milta hai.
